<section id="about" class="section">
<h2> About Us </h2>
<p>
M.M.E.S Women's Arts and Science College, established in 2007, is a Minority Institution
affiliated with Thiruvalluvar University and ISO certified. The college offers 12 undergraduate, 7
postgraduate, and 2 research programmes, serving over 2050 students with a team of 92 qualified
faculty members.
M.M.E.S provides a vibrant learning environment with modern infrastructure, digital library facilities,
remedial coaching, and value-added courses like NCVRT, DMLT, and Tally ERP 9. Its Career
Guidance and Placement Cell actively supports students in securing placements. With active units such
as NSS, YRC, and RRC, the college promotes holistic development through academics, research, and
community engagement.
</p>
